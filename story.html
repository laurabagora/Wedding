Meet Nathan & Laura

The groom

Born in Glendale, Arizona, Nathan spent his early years in Arizona, Albuquerque, and Buffalo before returning to Arizona at age four. His love for baseball carried him through college. How it happened

Our Favorite Chapters

01

Northern Arizona University · 2025

How We Met

We both attended NAU. Laura worked as a software developer at the university, and Nathan was a personal trainer at a nearby physio shop. Our workplaces were right across from each other. We exchanged smiles, but nerves kept us from saying hello. Thankfully, we connected on Hinge and went on our first date on February 22, 2025. We made it official on March 26.

02

Arizona to Florida

Driving Across the Country

After graduating, we adopted Megatron, packed up Laura’s eight year old Jeep (Brittany) and taught Nathan to drive a manual. Then the four of us set off on a 40 hour road trip to Melbourne, Florida. We made a home in our cozy one bedroom apartment, even surviving the adventure of assembling furniture together.

03

All the days in between

Making Memories Together in Florida

There have been baseball games in Phoenix and San Diego, theme parks, trips to Boston and Arizona, and more beach days than we can count. Each new place gave us another story to tell and another reason to be grateful we found each other.

04

The Bahamas · July 1, 2026

The Easiest Yes

During a cruise to the Bahamas, Nathan got down on one knee and asked Laura to spend forever with him. She said yes! Now we can’t wait to celebrate the next chapter with the family and friends who have been part of our story.
